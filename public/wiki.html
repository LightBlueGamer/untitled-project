Adventurer's handbook

Game guide

How to earn, grow, and explore your player record.

Getting started

Chat in a server where the bot is active to earn experience and money. Message rewards have a 30-second cooldown per player. Leveling up grants skill points, which you can spend in your skill tree.

Use “Add to your account” on the homepage to install the app to your Discord account. Its public slash commands can then be used in servers, the bot's DM, and DMs or group DMs with friends. Only you can invoke your installed commands; other people can see public replies such as scavenging results. Chat rewards and companion encounters still require an active server.

Visit the leaderboard to browse player records. Sign in with Discord to open your own profile and manage your progression.

Skills and progression

Sign in and visit your profile to choose your display name. It appears on the leaderboard and in game announcements, while your Discord username remains visible beneath it.

The profile's Boosts tab lists active temporary effects alongside unlocked skills, prestige bonuses, equipped companions, and tools. Open or refresh it after changing an effect or equipment. The overview shows ordinary message reward multipliers and the rounded bonus above the multiplied base, including skill, companion, and prestige effects. The rounded bonus can vary with the base roll. Conditional message-skill rewards can add more and are listed separately in Boosts.

The skill tree contains upgrades for experience, money, skill points, and companion encounters. Skills can have prerequisites, so follow the highlighted paths to see what you can unlock next. In Discord, use /skills to browse skills whose prerequisites are met, including those you cannot yet afford. Select a skill and confirm to spend its points.

The skill balance chart beneath the tree shows the fraction of skills unlocked in each category, not the strength of their effects. Drag the tree, use its zoom controls, or focus it and use the arrow keys to explore.

XP required to advance uses the base level curve multiplied by 1 for levels 1–10, 2 for levels 11–20, 3 for levels 21–30, and so on. Existing levels, skill points, and accumulated XP are retained when the curve changes.

When you reach the level requirement, use /prestige in Discord to reset your level, experience, unlocked skills, and unspent skill points in exchange for a prestige level and a choice of buff. Your prestige level increases future requirements.

Companions

Companions can appear while players chat. Catch one by pressing the button on its encounter message before it expires. Companions provide bonuses while equipped, and some skills improve companion encounter chances.

Use /companion list to view your collection, /companion equip to equip one, and /companion unequip to free a slot.

Browse the companion circulation to see species and rarity counts, or open a companion's entry for its stats and encounter odds.

Items, scavenging, and crafting

Use /scavenge to find an item and earn XP based on its rarity, price, and quantity. Searches have a five-minute base cooldown; Scavenging Efficiency reduces it. Seasoned Scavenger and your normal XP bonuses improve scavenging XP. The item catalog lists materials, components, consumables, treasures, and tools, including each item's rarity, price, description, and recipe when it can be crafted. Your profile shows your inventory and equipped tools.

Scavenging weights are relative selection weights, not percentages: an item with a larger weight is more likely to be selected than one with a smaller weight in the same pool. Rarity and tool modifiers affect those weights. Some items can be found without a tool, while an item's blacklist excludes it when a listed tool is used.

Items become less likely to appear as more players hold them. Use /tool equip to equip a tool; by default you can equip one at a time. Tool-slot skills can increase that limit. Use /use with a consumable to gain a temporary scavenging bonus. Tools can improve the chance of a specific category or of items at or above a rarity threshold. Open an item in the catalog to see its crafting ingredients and tool rules.

Item detail pages link to each crafting ingredient and to recipes that use the item, with the quantity needed for each recipe.

Catalog chances are approximate, per search, and use current circulation without tools or bonuses. The scavenging result shows your actual chance for that search, including equipped tools and active bonuses; an extra-find effect may award a second copy of the selected item.

Everyone starts with a Small Pouch holding 10 items. Each individual item, including equipped tools, takes one slot; the equipped backpack itself does not. Your profile shows used and total slots. Craft the Shoulder Bag, Travel Pack, Explorer's Backpack, and Adventurer's Rucksack in order to increase capacity to 20, 35, 55, and 80 slots. Each upgrade replaces the previous equipped backpack and consumes the other ingredients in its recipe.

A full backpack prevents scavenging without starting a cooldown, and extra finds are limited to the remaining space. Use /sell or sign in and sell items from your profile inventory at their listed price, or use consumables to make room. Selling the last copy of an equipped tool unequips it. Existing inventories over capacity are preserved, but must be reduced below capacity before scavenging again. Backpack circulation includes equipped backpacks.

Lootboxes

Each message eligible for the 30-second message reward has a 1 in 500 base chance to award a lootbox; higher-rarity boxes are progressively rarer. Every completed prestige also awards a box. At prestige 1, Rare is the most likely prestige box, and later prestiges improve the chance of higher rarities.

Use /lootbox list to view your boxes and boosts, /lootbox open to open a box, and /lootbox boost to activate a boost. Each box gives one item (45%), companion (45%), or boost (10%). Its rarity multiplies the weight of matching-rarity item and companion rewards by 20; other rarities are still possible. Items include scavengable and crafted items, except backpacks, which must be upgraded in order.

Boxes and their boosts are stored separately and do not use backpack slots. If an item reward cannot fit, it is saved in that box until you free a slot; it cannot be rerolled. Boosts randomly improve box drop chance, box rarity, reward rarity, or item and companion reward odds for 15, 30, or 60 minutes. Activating a second boost of the same type multiplies its effect and extends its remaining duration. Box Hunter, Box Fortune, Box Treasures, Treasure Recovery, and lootbox prestige buffs improve those same chances. Higher-rarity modifiers scale relative rarity weights per tier, not guaranteed drops. Prestige uses your bonuses immediately before resetting skills.

Casino

The casino has Plinko, Blackjack, Pass the Bills, poker, multiplayer lobbies, and casino statistics. Wagers use your player balance; check each game's rules before placing a bet. Equipped casino companions, casino skills, and permanent prestige buffs increase winning payouts in Plinko, Blackjack, Pass the Bills, and solo poker against the house. The casino page shows your active boost and adjusted Plinko winning multipliers. These bonuses do not affect losses, ties, win odds, or games against other players.

Auction House

Visit the store to list items, companions, unopened lootboxes and unused boosts. Backpacks and lootboxes with saved rewards cannot be listed. The daily listing fee is 1% of the starting bid (minimum $10), charged when listed and every 24 hours thereafter. If it cannot be paid, the auction ends and the seller is notified on their profile.

Auctions last between one hour and seven days. Bids are held until outbid or settled; each new bid must exceed the previous one by at least 5% or $1. Ending early awards the current highest bidder. Winners and sellers claim assets from the store; items need enough backpack space.

There are three promoted slots. A 24-hour promotion costs at least $100 or 1% of starting price, whichever is greater. If all slots are occupied, pay more than the lowest payment to replace it. Unused promotion time is refunded when replaced or when the auction ends.

Bot Shop

The Bot Shop offers a shared, random selection of items for 24 hours. Rarer items and items already common in circulation are less likely to appear. The developer may restock early from the Store or developer dashboard. Backpacks are not sold.

Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic and Legendary items begin with 10, 7, 5, 2 and 1 stock respectively. Mythic and Eternal begin at −1 and −3: randomly generated extra stock must bring their total above zero before they can appear. Higher quantities are increasingly unlikely, especially for rarer items.

Each item's price is rounded to the nearest dollar from a random 2.5×–100× its base price, weighted toward the higher end. Lower circulation also shifts the price upward. Stock is shared: sold-out listings remain visible until the next restock, and purchases require sufficient balance and backpack space.

Each restock guarantees one item; each additional item becomes less likely. Search and filter the shop by rarity or item type. Signed-in players receive a website alert for new selections, which can be marked read. The public purchase history shows buyers, items, quantities and prices for 30 days, starting when purchase recording was added.

Commands

  • /help — show a quick guide and link to this page.
  • /skills — browse available skills and confirm unlocks.
  • /prestige — prestige when you meet the level requirement.
  • /companion list, /companion equip, and /companion unequip — manage your companions.
  • /scavenge — search for an item and earn XP (five-minute base cooldown).
  • /sell — sell items for their listed price and free backpack space.
  • /lootbox list, /lootbox open, and /lootbox boost — manage boxes and their boosts.
  • /tool equip, /tool unequip, and /tool list — manage scavenging tools.
  • /use — consume an item for a temporary bonus.
