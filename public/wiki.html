Adventurer's handbook

Game guide

How to earn, grow, and explore your player record.

Getting started

Chat in a server where the bot is active to earn experience and money. Message rewards have a 30-second cooldown per player. Leveling up grants skill points, which you can spend in your skill tree.

Visit the leaderboard to browse player records. Sign in with Discord to open your own profile and manage your progression.

Skills and progression

The skill tree contains upgrades for experience, money, skill points, and companion encounters. Skills can have prerequisites, so follow the highlighted paths to see what you can unlock next.

When you reach the level requirement, use /prestige in Discord to reset your level, experience, unlocked skills, and unspent skill points in exchange for a prestige level and a choice of buff. Your prestige level increases future requirements.

Companions

Companions can appear while players chat. Catch one by pressing the button on its encounter message before it expires. Companions provide bonuses while equipped, and some skills improve companion encounter chances.

Use /companion list to view your collection, /companion equip to equip one, and /companion unequip to free a slot.

Browse the companion circulation to see species and rarity counts, or open a companion's entry for its stats and encounter odds.

Casino

The casino has Plinko, Blackjack, Pass the Bills, poker, multiplayer lobbies, and casino statistics. Wagers use your player balance; check each game's rules before placing a bet.

Commands

  • /help — show a quick guide and link to this page.
  • /prestige — prestige when you meet the level requirement.
  • /companion list, /companion equip, and /companion unequip — manage your companions.
