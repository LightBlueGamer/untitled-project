How to play
  • Everyone at the table puts down 3 bills worth your bill value. Your total bet is 3 × the bill value.
  • On your turn you roll one die per bill you hold (max 3). Faces: ⚪ circle = keep the bill, ⬅️ left = pass it to the left, ➡️ right = pass it to the right, ⭐ star = put it in the centre (out of play).
  • Play goes around the circle until only one bill is left.
  • The holder of the last bill must roll a circle to win the whole pot. Left/right passes the bill on, a star is a re-roll.
  • If you win you get the pot times a multiplier that grows with the table size: 3 players 1×, 4 players 1.25×, 5 players 1.5×, 6 players 1.75×, 7 players 2×, 8 players 4×.
  • If anyone else wins, you lose your bet. Tick "Auto roll" to roll your dice automatically.

How to play
  • Get a hand closer to 21 than the dealer without going over.
  • Number cards count their value, J/Q/K count 10, and an Ace counts 11 or 1, whichever is better.
  • Hit to take another card, Stand to keep your hand, or Double down to double your bet, take exactly one more card and stand.
  • The dealer draws until reaching 17 or more and stands on all 17s.
  • Win pays 1:1, a natural blackjack (21 with two cards) pays 3:2, and a tie returns your bet. Going over 21 loses. There is no splitting.

How to play
  • Set a bet per ball, how many balls to drop (1–50), and press Drop balls. Each ball falls through the pegs and lands in a slot at the bottom.
  • The slot's multiplier is multiplied by your bet per ball. Below 1× loses part of your bet, above 1× wins.
  • Risk changes the payouts: low has small swings, high has big payouts at the edges and tiny ones in the middle.
  • More rows raise the top payout. The fewest rows give 25% of the top multiplier, 16 rows give 100% (20× on high risk).
  • Auto drop keeps dropping one ball at a time at the chosen speed until you stop it or run out of money.
