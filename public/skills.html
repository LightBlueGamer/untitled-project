A guide to your journey

Exploring the tree

Hover over a skill to see its effect; select it to unlock it. Gain one skill point per level before skill bonuses.

Glowing nodes are unlocked; colored outlines are available; grey nodes are locked or unaffordable.

Base skills line up in the center and branch outward. Hover, focus or select a skill to highlight its full prerequisite path.

Experience skills are green, income skills are gold, skill point skills are red, and house-game payout skills are orange.

Drag the tree to explore and use the mouse wheel or zoom buttons to zoom. On a keyboard, focus the tree and use the arrow keys, or tab through skills.
